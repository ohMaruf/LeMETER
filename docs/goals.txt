Dataset: NYU Depth V2, KITTI.
Task: Comparative study of a lightweight MDE architecture (METER/MobileViT) pre-trained with LeJEPA vs.
ImageNet-Supervised pre-training, focusing on depth accuracy and feature interpretability.
Main objectives:
• Lightweight LeJEPA Integration: Implement the LeJEPA training objective (SIGReg) on a mobile-friendly
backbone (e.g., the ViT-lite used in METER). The goal is to perform self-supervised pre-training on static
images with minimal computational resources (single GPU).
• Zero-shot Geometric Probing: Before adding the depth decoder, analyze the latent space using PCA. Evaluate
if the ”free” semantic segments emerging from LeJEPA align with depth discontinuities (e.g., if the PCA
map naturally separates foreground objects from the background).
• MDE Downstream Performance: Fine-tune the LeJEPA-METER model for depth estimation. Compare the
results against the original supervised METER in terms of RMSE, AbsRel, and δ1 metrics, verifying if SSL
pre-training speeds up convergence.
• Efficiency-Accuracy Trade-off: Quantify the performance gains relative to the training cost. Analyze if
LeJEPA’s ”heuristics-free” approach (no teacher-student, no stop-gradient) makes it more suitable for stu-
dents to adapt MDE models to specific robotic domains with limited data.
